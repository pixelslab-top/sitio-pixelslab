Publish videos to TikTok for Mia Valeska, Overtime Horror and Tales Too Dark — straight from the web.

Mia Valeska Uploader is the publishing web app for a small family of content projects run by a single creator: Mia Valeska (@miiia.valeska) and the horror storytelling channels Overtime Horror and Tales Too Dark. It signs in with TikTok's official Login Kit and sends videos through TikTok's official Content Posting API — either as drafts in the creator's TikTok inbox or as direct posts to the connected account.

The button opens TikTok's own authorization page. You review the permissions there and approve them on TikTok — this site never sees or asks for your TikTok password.

How it works

Three steps, all of them running on TikTok's official developer platform.

1

Connect with TikTok

The account owner signs in through TikTok Login Kit and authorizes this app for one of the projects' accounts. TikTok returns an authorization code to this site's callback page.

2

Choose a video

The creator picks the finished video file and its title. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except TikTok itself.

3

Draft or direct post

The Content Posting API sends the video to the connected account — as an inbox draft to review and publish in the TikTok app, or posted directly to the profile.

Two ways to publish

Upload as draft

The video lands in the creator's TikTok inbox as an unpublished draft notification. The creator opens it in the TikTok app, checks it, and publishes it manually.

This is the default, review-first mode.

Direct post

The video is published straight to the connected account's profile with its title, using the account's own privacy settings chosen at posting time.

Used only when the creator chooses it for a specific video.

Permissions the app requests

The app asks TikTok for exactly three scopes — the minimum needed to identify the account and publish its videos.

ScopeWhat it allows
user.info.basic Read the connected account's basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) so the app can confirm it is connected to the right account.
video.upload Upload video files to the connected account as drafts, delivered through the creator's TikTok inbox.
video.publish Publish videos directly to the connected account's profile through the Content Posting API.

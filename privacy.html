Privacy Policy

Mia Valeska Uploader · Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Mia Valeska Uploader web app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is a publishing tool that uploads videos to the TikTok accounts of the operator's content projects — Mia Valeska (@miiia.valeska) and the horror channels Overtime Horror and Tales Too Dark — using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated by the owner of the Mia Valeska, Overtime Horror and Tales Too Dark TikTok accounts. For any privacy question, contact us at mia.valeska011@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information. When you connect the App with TikTok, TikTok shares basic profile information allowed by the user.info.basic scope: your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar image. We use this only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Authorization data. When you authorize the App, TikTok returns an authorization code, which is exchanged for access and refresh tokens. These tokens are what allow the App to upload videos on the account's behalf.
  • Video content you provide. The video files and titles you choose to upload through the App. This content is sent directly to TikTok through the Content Posting API.

The App does not collect your TikTok password, and it cannot: sign-in happens entirely on TikTok's own authorization page. The App does not collect payment information, contacts, location data, or browsing history, and it does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use the information

  • To authenticate the connected TikTok account and confirm it is one of the operator's intended project accounts.
  • To upload videos to that account as inbox drafts or direct posts, exactly as the account owner requests.
  • To keep the connection working (for example, refreshing an access token before it expires).

We do not use the information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the account owner's own videos.

4. How information is shared

Information is shared only with TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs, as required for the App to function: profile information is read from TikTok, and videos and tokens are exchanged with TikTok's servers. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with any other third party.

5. Data retention and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored securely and kept only while the App remains connected to the TikTok account. Video files are transmitted to TikTok for posting and are not retained by the App after the upload completes, except where the account owner keeps their own copies. If the App's access is revoked in TikTok's settings, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access at any time. You can disconnect the App from TikTok's settings (Settings → Manage apps / authorized apps) or from your TikTok account's connected-apps page. Revoking access immediately stops the App from acting on your account.
  • Request deletion. To ask us to delete any stored authorization data associated with your account, email mia.valeska011@gmail.com and we will delete it and confirm by reply.

7. Children

The App is a creator publishing tool and is not directed at children. It is intended for use by the adult owner of the connected TikTok accounts.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data: mia.valeska011@gmail.com.
